Safety & Conduct

COMMUNITY GUIDELINES.

Every {{ site.site_name }} experience is built on respect, safety, and zero judgement.

1. 18+ Adults Only

All {{ site.site_name }} meetups, experiences, and trips are strictly for participants aged 18 and above.

2. Respect Boundaries & Consent

No harassment, unwanted advances, intrusive questioning, or pressure to share personal contact info or social handles.

3. Be Present

Show up on time, stay engaged with the group, and ask before taking photos or videos of other participants.

4. Zero Tolerance for Abuse

Any discriminatory, abusive, or unsafe behaviour results in immediate removal from the experience and permanent ban from the platform.

Need to report an issue? Report safely here or email {{ site.contact_email }}.

Data & Trust

PRIVACY POLICY.

We collect only what is needed to coordinate your experience safely.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an experience, we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, and optional accessibility/event notes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your contact details are used solely to confirm bookings, share meeting point details, and coordinate safety during trips or meetups. We never sell or rent your data.

3. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your booking notes or contact data anytime by emailing {{ site.contact_email }}.

Bookings & Refunds

CANCELLATION POLICY.

Clear, fair rules so hosts can plan small groups reliably.

1. Standard Meetups & Experiences

Cancel at least 24–48 hours before the start time (exact window shown on each experience page) for a full refund or free reschedule.

2. Day Trips & Travel Experiences

Trips involve shared transport and bookings. Unless specified otherwise on the trip page: full refund 5+ days before departure, 50% refund 3–4 days before, no refund within 48 hours.

3. If {{ site.site_name }} Cancels

If an event is cancelled due to weather, minimum group size, or host unavailability, you receive a 100% refund or priority transfer.

To cancel a booking, check My Plans and reach out with your reference number.
