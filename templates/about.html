Our Story

WE BELIEVE
GREAT STORIES
START UNEXPECTEDLY.

NXTUP is a social-experience community built around a simple idea: you shouldn't have to wait for your existing friend group to be available before you do something interesting.

We organise small meetups, shared activities, and local trips that give people an opportunity to meet new faces, explore new places, and step outside their everyday routine.

Come alone. Bring your curiosity. Discover what's next.
